About FoodShare

FoodShare is a food-sharing web application designed to help surplus food reach people who need it instead of being thrown away. Users can post food, discover available listings, request food, coordinate collection and review completed exchanges.

🎯

Our Mission

Make it simple for people and organisations to share surplus edible food responsibly and support their local communities.

🌱

Our Vision

A community where good food is valued, avoidable waste is reduced, and people can find support through local sharing.

🫶

Why FoodShare?

One place to publish surplus food, find nearby listings, manage requests, receive notifications and share feedback after an exchange.

How FoodShare Works

A simple flow that helps donors and receivers coordinate food sharing.

1

Create an account

Register as a donor to share food or as a receiver to find and request available food.

2

Share or request

Donors publish food details and pickup deadlines. Receivers browse listings and send requests.

3

Coordinate and review

Users manage request updates, coordinate collection, and submit ratings after a completed exchange.
